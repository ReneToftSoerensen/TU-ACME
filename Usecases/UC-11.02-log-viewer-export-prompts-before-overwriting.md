# UC-11.02 — Log viewer export prompts before overwriting

**Behavior:** When the operator chooses "Export to file" and supplies a destination path that already exists, the viewer prompts `y/N` defaulting to N. Anything other than `y`/`Y` cancels the export and no CSV is written.

**Given** the log viewer has rendered events and the operator selects "Export to file"
**When** the destination file already exists and the operator answers `n` (or empty) at the overwrite prompt
**Then** `Export-Csv` is NOT called and the viewer returns to its menu loop

**Implementation:** `TU-ACME/Private/Logs/Invoke-LogViewer.ps1`
**Test:** `tests/Unit/Logs/UC-11.02.Tests.ps1`
