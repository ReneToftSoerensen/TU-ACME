# UC-10.01 — Export PFX prompts overwrite and calls Export-PfxCertificate

**Behavior:** When the operator selects PFX export and the destination file already exists, the menu prompts `y/N` defaulting to N. If the answer is anything other than `y`/`Y`, `Export-PfxCertificate` is NOT called. On `y`, `Export-PfxCertificate` is invoked once with `-Cert "Cert:\LocalMachine\My\<thumbprint>"`, `-FilePath <path>`, and `-Password` as a `SecureString`. Event 1009 is emitted on success.

**Given** the export menu is open with at least one prod certificate listed
**When** the operator picks a cert, selects "PFX (password protected)", supplies a path that already exists, and answers `n` at the overwrite prompt
**Then** `Export-PfxCertificate` is NOT called

**Implementation:** `TU-ACME/Private/Export/Invoke-ExportMenu.ps1`
**Test:** `tests/Unit/Export/UC-10.01.Tests.ps1`
