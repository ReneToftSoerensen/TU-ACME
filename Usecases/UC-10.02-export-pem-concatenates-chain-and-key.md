# UC-10.02 — Export PEM concatenates chain and key

**Behavior:** When the operator selects PEM export, the menu resolves the chosen certificate via `Get-PACertificate -MainDomain <subject>`, reads `$cert.FullChainFile` and `$cert.KeyFile` from disk, and writes both contents concatenated to the destination path. No password is involved.

**Given** the export menu is open with at least one prod certificate listed
**When** the operator picks a cert, selects "PEM (no password)", and supplies a destination path
**Then** `Get-Content` is called for both the chain and the key files, and `Set-Content` writes the combined PEM to the destination

**Implementation:** `TU-ACME/Private/Export/Invoke-ExportMenu.ps1`
**Test:** `tests/Unit/Export/UC-10.02.Tests.ps1`
